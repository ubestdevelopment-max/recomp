    How to progress

    • Each week, add 1 to 2 reps per set.
    • Top of the range in every set? Slow down to 3 sec per rep, then go harder.
    • Push-ups: knee → regular → feet raised → diamond.
    • Squats: regular → slow → jump squats → split squats.
    • Test your max push-ups every 4 weeks.

    Meals

    Creatine

    • 3 to 5 g every day, rest days included.
    • Always after food. Never on an empty stomach.
    • With water or juice. In coffee is fine; drink it within 15 to 30 min.
    • Headache? Drop to 3 g and drink more water. Still there after a few days, stop for 3 to 5 days to test.
